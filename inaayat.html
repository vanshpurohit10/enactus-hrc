The problem

41% of girls don't know about periods until they start.

Around 41% of girls are unaware of menstruation before their first period, and limited access to hygienic products leads to unsafe practices and infections.

The waste

Over a billion pads thrown away. Every month.

Each one can take 500 years or more to decompose.

How it works

Two halves of one project.

01 · STITCH

Made by women, for women

Reusable sanitary kits are stitched by women from underserved communities, so every kit is also a livelihood.

02 · PARTNER

Schools and NGOs

We work with schools and NGOs who know the community, from Mumbai's municipal schools to villages in Palghar.

03 · TALK

The session

A menstrual hygiene session: the biology, hygiene practices, myths against facts, and a space to ask anything.

04 · HAND OVER

Kits at the session

Kits are given out at the session itself, with a picture guide on how to use and care for them.

05 · FUND

Orders pay for reach

Paying orders fund production, while partnerships carry the kits to communities where access is hardest.

What's in the kit

Everything she needs, in one pouch.

The original Inaayat kit, made with Days for Girls: two shields, eight absorbent liners, a menstrual chart, a pictorial guide and two soaps.

Kits change with the community. For Give Her the Wings 3.0 each kit held three reusable pads, an instruction chart in Marathi and a bar of soap.

Business model

Three ways a kit reaches someone.

Paying orders fund production, while partnerships carry our reach to communities where access is hardest.

01 · COMMUNITY

Distribution with NGOs

Kits go out through NGO partners, at sessions, to the people who need them most.

02 · B2B

Corporates and institutions

Partnerships and bulk orders, like the ₹4.15 lakh order for 2,600 kits from Bindu in 2021.

03 · D2C

Direct to you

Anyone can buy a kit, and every sale keeps the production centre stitching.

Our flagship campaign

Give Her the Wings.

Once a year we raise funds, prepare a thousand kits and take sessions to villages where most women have never had a reusable option.

September 2024 · Charoti, Dahanu

Where 80% had no proper facilities

A village visit found that 80% of women in Charoti lacked adequate sanitary facilities. A fundraiser at college raised over ₹2 lakh, and 54 members, with faculty and student volunteers, took 1,000 reusable kits, 2,000 innerwear items and sessions to the village.

Our journey

From one idea in 2018 to a national stage.

  1. Apr 2018

    Inaayat begins

    Launched as India's first chapter under Days for Girls International, to make reusable menstrual hygiene kits and run awareness sessions.

  2. 2019

    On the national stage

    The Inaayat menstrual hygiene campaign, and Inaayat presented at Enactus India Nationals 2019 alongside Aajeevika.

  3. 2020

    Let's talk period

    Periodicals, an IGTV series on menstrual health, and Period Party, a month of webinars with founders and menstrual coaches.

  4. 2020 – 21

    2nd of 105 projects

    Runner-up at the Youth Innovation Challenge hosted by Red Dot Foundation–Safecity, with UN-Habitat and UNICEF among its partners.

    Red Dot Foundation
  5. May 2021

    2,600 kits for Bindu

    A ₹4.15 lakh order: 2,600 kits from our rural production centre, distributed across North and Central India.

  6. Sep 2022

    National Award

    Best Social Initiative for Women Empowerment at Bhavishya Bharat, among 186 colleges, for Inaayat and Aajeevika.

    National Award
  7. Sep 2024

    Give Her the Wings, Charoti

    1,000 reusable kits and 2,000 innerwear items for a village where 80% of women lacked adequate sanitary facilities.

  8. Jan – Feb 2025

    Nagpur

    1,200 reusable sanitary pads donated at a tribal police camp in Nagpur.

  9. Sep 2025

    Give Her the Wings 3.0

    1,000 kits and sessions across Dhaniwari, Kasa and Murbad in Palghar, covered by Mid-day in October.

  10. Feb 2026

    Sakhi drive, Colaba

    A menstrual hygiene session in Colaba with Think India HRC.

  11. Mar 2026

    Top 4 at IIT Roorkee

    Inaayat placed in the Top 4 of the Mature Stage at Envision, E-Summit '26.

    Envision · IIT Roorkee
  12. Oct 2026

    Give Her the Wings 2026

    The flagship campaign, back in the villages.
