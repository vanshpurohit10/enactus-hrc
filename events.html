Where 80% had no proper facilities

A village visit found that 80% of women in Charoti lacked adequate sanitary facilities. A fundraiser at college raised over ₹2 lakh, and 54 members, with faculty and student volunteers, took 1,000 reusable kits, 2,000 innerwear items and sessions to the village.

Our flagship entrepreneurship conclave

Synapse.

SYNAPSE is the flagship entrepreneurship conclave organised by Enactus H.R. College of Commerce and Economics, bringing together aspiring entrepreneurs, industry professionals, innovators and young changemakers on a common platform.

Follow @enactushrc for event updates

Panel discussions

The conclave creates an engaging space for thought-provoking panel discussions, where accomplished speakers share their insights, experiences and perspectives on entrepreneurship, sustainability and the evolving business landscape.

The flagship competition

At its heart lies SYNAPSE's flagship competition, which challenges participants to think creatively, approach real-world social challenges with an entrepreneurial mindset and present innovative ideas with conviction.

Why it matters

Through meaningful conversations, intellectual exchange and a spirit of healthy competition, SYNAPSE aims to inspire fresh perspectives, foster collaboration and empower the next generation of leaders to turn ideas into meaningful impact.
