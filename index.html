What we believe

We believe in engaging students who take entrepreneurial action for others, to create a better world for us all.

How we work

Not charity. A business that lasts.

We work with communities at the grassroots and build sustainable business models with them. Our projects have reached more than seven states.

Find a need

Visit the community first. Our Charoti visit found 80% of women without adequate sanitary facilities.

Build the model

Design a product or service that pays for itself: kits, credits, products people want to buy.

Run it together

The community does the work and earns from it: stitching kits, recovering plastic.

Measure and grow

Count the kilos, the kits and the lives, then take it further each year.

Where we've been

Seventeen years, one idea at a time.

2009 – 2012 · OUR FIRST PROJECT

Chirag

"Light for All": differently-abled communities assembled, tested and maintained solar lamps for villages without grid power. It took us to the Enactus World Cup 2012, 3rd among 36 nations, and became the Chirag Rural Development Foundation.

11,918houses lit across 297 villages
2012 – 2015

Aasha

Trained women from underprivileged communities as skilled beauty professionals, so they could earn for their families.

50+women trained
2015

The Peach Vibe

Aasha's next phase: workplace wellness sessions for companies, delivered by women from the community as massage therapists.

2-in-1less stress at work, more jobs for women
EARLIER

Saarthi

Literacy and English proficiency for underprivileged communities.

ABClanguage as a way up
EARLIER

Jalvruddhi

Low-cost water filters for rural families, sold through community champions trained in the village, with sessions on water conservation.

H₂Oclean water, run by the village
LIVELIHOOD

Aajeevika

"Livelihood": rural women of Maharashtra trained as seamstresses, making zero-waste bags, scrunchies and pouches from fabric cut-offs.

2,000bags sold
2024 – 25

Samudra Manthan

Recycling ghost nets and supporting Mumbai's fishing communities. Its waste-offsetting pilots grew into Project Sanchay.

Best InnovationEnvision 2025, IIT Roorkee
